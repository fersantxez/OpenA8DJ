# Brand and Risk Policy

This policy is project guidance, not legal advice.

## Independence

OpenA8DJ is an independent community compatibility project. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, certified by, or supported by Native
Instruments.

Native Instruments, Audio 8 DJ, Traktor, and other product names are trademarks
of their respective owners. Their names are used only to identify compatibility
with the relevant hardware and software.

## OpenA8DJ Name

The MIT License covers the source code. It does not grant permission to imply
that modified builds, repackaged installers, mirrors, forks, support services,
or commercial offerings are official OpenA8DJ releases.

You may use the OpenA8DJ name to refer truthfully to this project. If you
publish modified builds or redistribute installers, make the source and status
clear. Do not present modified or repackaged builds as official releases from
the OpenA8DJ maintainers.

Preferred wording:

```text
Based on OpenA8DJ, an independent compatibility project.
```

Avoid wording such as:

```text
Official OpenA8DJ installer
Certified Native Instruments Audio 8 DJ driver
Native Instruments OpenA8DJ driver
```

## Native Instruments Marks

Do not use Native Instruments logos, product artwork, screenshots, trade dress,
or marketing assets as OpenA8DJ branding or installer artwork.

Compatibility statements should be factual and limited, for example:

```text
OpenA8DJ is an independent compatibility driver for the Native Instruments
Audio 8 DJ USB audio interface.
```

## Official Downloads

The official OpenA8DJ source repository and public binary releases are published
from:

```text
https://github.com/fersantxez/OpenA8DJ
```

GitHub release assets are the only official public binary downloads from this
project. Mirrors, forks, third-party installers, and repackaged builds are not
official unless the OpenA8DJ maintainers explicitly say so.

## Donations

Optional donations or coffee links are voluntary thank-you gestures. They do
not buy a license, warranty, support entitlement, certification, endorsement, or
commercial relationship.

## Use At Your Own Risk

OpenA8DJ is provided as-is, without warranty. Driver software can affect system
audio, USB device behavior, DJ workflows, and live performance reliability.
Install and use it at your own risk.

Public preview builds are not certified by Native Instruments, Apple,
Microsoft, or any DJ software vendor. Do not rely on preview or experimental
builds for paid events, production shows, or critical systems unless you have
personally tested and accepted the risk.
